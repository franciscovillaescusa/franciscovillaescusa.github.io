Welcome to the HADES simulations!

The HADES simulations are a set of more than 1,000 N-body and hydrodynamic simulations with massless and massive neutrinos. They were designed to understand the impact of neutrino masses on cosmological observables in the fully non-linear regime.

HADES is now part of the Quijote simulations. Please see the Quijote documentation for details about the simulations, how to access the data, and how to get help.
